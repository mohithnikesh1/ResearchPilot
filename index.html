How your information is used

Your manuscript, dataset description and chat messages may be sent to OpenAI to provide AI assistance. OpenAlex receives subject keywords to identify suitable journals using related article evidence. Policy checks send journal identifiers or the DOI to the relevant services. Describe sensitive datasets without sharing their contents. Contact UW–Madison Library for help with confidential research.
