Publishing and research data help for UW–Madison researchers

Find journals that suit your manuscript, check which version of a published article you may share, and choose a repository for your dataset. Each result names the source it came from.

ResearchPilot is an independent tool. It is not an official UW–Madison or UW–Madison Libraries service.

Where the answers come from

ResearchPilot looks up journal details in a fixed database and sharing policies in named policy services. The AI reads what you enter, chooses from those records and writes the explanations. Policies, fees and agreements change, so confirm them with the publisher or UW–Madison Libraries before you submit or deposit.

How your information is used

Your manuscript, dataset description and chat messages may be sent to OpenAI to provide AI assistance. OpenAlex receives subject keywords to identify suitable journals using related article evidence. Policy checks send journal identifiers or the DOI to the relevant services. Describe sensitive datasets without sharing their contents. Contact UW–Madison Library for help with confidential research.

How would you like to check sharing permissions?

Check a specific article or explore a journal's sharing policy.

Check article-sharing permissions

Review which version you may share, where you may deposit it, and any conditions that apply.

Paste the DOI or the article's web address. ResearchPilot finds the DOI and checks that exact article. When a MINDS@UW snapshot is loaded, it also checks whether the article is already there.
Journal-level results come directly from the Jisc Open Policy Finder API and may contain several conditional options. They do not replace an article-level DOI check.
