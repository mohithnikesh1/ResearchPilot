Strategy & compliance

🛡️ Check journal Green OA / self-archiving policy

Version-specific deposit permissions, embargoes, and licence conditions — with deposit guidance for MINDS@UW, ShareYourPaper, and Zenodo.

💡 Best result: enter your article's DOI or URL. With a DOI, the answer is verified against the OA.Works Permissions database (the dataset behind cOAlition S's Journal Checker Tool) — not AI-generated. Without one, you'll get a journal-level estimate marked "Not confirmed".

🗄️ Find a research data repository

Describe your dataset and get matched to domain-specific and generalist repositories from a curated, verified registry. UW-Madison researchers: MINDS@UW and Dryad are free of charge and always listed first.
